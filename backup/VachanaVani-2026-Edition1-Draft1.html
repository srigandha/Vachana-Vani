ವಚನ · Vachana
ಹೊಯ್ದಡೆ ಹೊಯ್ಗಳು ಕೈಯ ಮೇಲೆ, ಬೈದಡೆ ಬೈಗಳು ಕೈಯ ಮೇಲೆ. ಹಿಂದಣ ಜನನವೇದಡಾಗಲಿ, ಇಂದಿನ ಭೋಗವು ಕೈಯ ಮೇಲೆ. ಕೂಡಲಸಂಗಮದೇವಯ್ಯಾ ನಿಮ್ಮ ಪೂಜಿಸಿದ ಫಲ ಕೈಯ ಮೇಲೆ.
About Basavanna
12th-century social reformer, philosopher, and statesman. Founder of the Lingayat movement and master of the vachana tradition.
ಅರ್ಥ · Line-by-Line Meaning
ಹೊಯ್ದಡೆ ಹೊಯ್ಗಳು ಕೈಯ ಮೇಲೆ
If you strike someone, the act of striking rests on your own hands — you bear the consequence of violence.
ಬೈದಡೆ ಬೈಗಳು ಕೈಯ ಮೇಲೆ
If you curse or abuse another, those very words return to burden your own hands — your speech defines you.
ಹಿಂದಣ ಜನನವೇದಡಾಗಲಿ, ಇಂದಿನ ಭೋಗವು ಕೈಯ ಮೇಲೆ
Whatever your past birth may have been, the fruits of your present deeds and experiences are entirely in your hands today.
ಕೂಡಲಸಂಗಮದೇವಯ್ಯಾ, ನಿಮ್ಮ ಪೂಜಿಸಿದ ಫಲ ಕೈಯ ಮೇಲೆ
O Lord Kudala Sangama, the reward of worshipping you and living righteously is immediate — it is right here, in our own hands.
Core Theme (ತಿರುಳು)
Personal accountability — every action, word, and deed creates its own immediate result in this very life.
Spiritual Insight (ಆಧ್ಯಾತ್ಮಿಕ ಒಳನೋಟ)
Basavanna rejects fatalism. Past births do not bind us — righteous living brings its own fruit, here and now.
